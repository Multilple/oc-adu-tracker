Step 3 · Result
Fits
    Checked: inside the lot, at least 4 ft from the side and back lot lines, not touching the house.
    Santa Ana's 15 ft house separation (Table 41-194.3) can be reduced for ADUs up to 800 sq ft, which covers all 10 designs.
    Not checked: front setback, easements, utilities, flood zone, historic district.
